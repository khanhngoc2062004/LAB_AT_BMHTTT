# Paper Comparison

## Original Study

Title: Software security in practice: knowledge and motivation

DOI: 10.1093/cybsec/tyaf005

Method: Semi-structured interviews and qualitative coding.

Participants: 13 professional developers.

## Our Demo

Dataset: 8 synthetic interview statements.

Open Coding: 8 codes.

Axial Coding: 3 categories.

Selective Coding: 1 core category.

Knowledge Acquisition: 3 learning types.

Motivation Analysis: 4 illustrative categories.

## Similarities

Both follow a qualitative coding framework.

Both examine security knowledge and motivation.

## Differences

The original study uses real interview data.

Our demo uses synthetic statements.

Our categories are simplified and not identical
to the original research taxonomy.

## Limitations

1. No access to original interview transcripts.
2. Small synthetic dataset.
3. No real participant recruitment.
4. No independent qualitative coders.
5. No assessment of data saturation.
6. No validation of the original taxonomy.

## Conclusion

This demo reproduces selected qualitative analysis
steps but does not reproduce the original empirical findings.
